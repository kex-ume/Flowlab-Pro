Volumetric Flow Rate Coveter

Convert a flow rate between common laboratory, field, process and petroleum units. Results are calculated locally and are not stored as controlled records.

Converted flow rate—Enter a value to convert.

Conversion basis

Every unit is normalized through cubic metres per second.

Petroleum barrel conversions use 1 bbl = 0.158987294928 m³. US and Imperial gallons are treated as different units. This utility converts volumetric flow rate only; mass-flow conversion requires fluid density and is intentionally excluded.
